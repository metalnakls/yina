# yina 6

- Coordinate Watch Together playback against the host’s clock over a persistent local connection.
- Send playback controls independently of shared-drive progress saves.
- Pause the room while a participant seeks or buffers, then schedule playback together when everyone is ready.
- Correct small playback drift with bounded rate adjustments instead of periodic corrective seeks.
- Enable shared rooms on SMB servers that do not support exclusive rename or hard links, and show setup errors directly.
- Use one update channel, including compatibility for older update-feed URLs.

Both Macs need version 6 for the new Watch Together protocol. Shared media and saved progress remain on the drive; live control traffic goes directly between the Macs.

Synchronization targets the players’ media timelines. Independent display refresh and audio-output clocks mean identical physical frame presentation and sample-accurate sound across Macs are not guaranteed.
