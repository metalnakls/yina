# yina 3

This major release adds shared-folder Watch Together, improves playback responsiveness, and updates subtitle, Picture in Picture, and HDR controls.

- **Watch Together:** share a host's queue, follow play/pause, seeks and speed, and move between episodes. Missing or incomplete episodes wait until they are available. Local controls and Leave Watch Together return control to you.
- **One show metadata folder:** `.yina/` holds inventory, progress, queues and requests. Recognized legacy `.yina.json` files migrate while preserving identity and inventory bytes.
- **Glass download requests:** see whether a request is pending, accepted or failed, then explicitly retry or withdraw it. Acceptance does not imply that the episode has finished downloading. Requires a compatible Glass build.
- **Playback responsiveness:** asynchronous playback controls and UI snapshots; background media preparation, history and cache work; bounded shared-folder and plugin I/O; improved preview cancellation and idle-player cleanup; updated AVFoundation lifecycle handling.
- **Subtitles and tracks:** subtitle scaling follows the displayed video image, with explicit audio/video/subtitle switches and no duplicate primary/secondary subtitle selection.
- **Picture in Picture:** supported plain-text subtitles can appear beneath the PiP window. Host-window hide/restore is simplified, and entering PiP preserves the existing fullscreen Space. Styled and image subtitles retain native video rendering.
- **Playback UI:** floating controls anchor to the video image, and pause/seek overlays, playback controls and sidebars follow HDR headroom while retaining adaptive colors.

## Validation

Local protocol, migration, menu, subtitle and playback fixtures pass. A generated-media test with two real libmpv engines covers transport synchronization, episode changes, local takeover and host stop. Builds and signed update archives are verified by the release workflow.

Real SMB use between Macs, perceived HDR brightness and native PiP/Spaces transitions still need runtime testing. The original intermittent resume beachball has not been confirmed eliminated. Watch Together uses shared-file polling rather than frame-accurate streaming.

Apple silicon; macOS 27 or later.
